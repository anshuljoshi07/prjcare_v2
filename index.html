Our Mission

What We Stand For

PRJ Care Foundation is dedicated to empowering underprivileged communities through education, health, and holistic development across Dwarka, New Delhi.

Education

Free basic education classes for underprivileged children in Dwarka. We believe every child deserves to learn, grow, and thrive.

Community

Building stronger communities through festivals, celebrations, and cultural events that bring people together across all backgrounds.

Environment

Plantation drives, Say No to Plastic campaigns, and Earth Day awareness — protecting our planet for future generations.

Health

Yoga sessions, cancer awareness campaigns, and health drives in partnership with Manipal Hospital, Dwarka — wellness for all.
