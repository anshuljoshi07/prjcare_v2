Since 2017, we have been laying the foundation for a better India — one child, one community, one act of kindness at a time.

Who We Are

A Legacy of Love & Service

"Join us in laying the foundation for a bright future of our country."

EKUM is a New Delhi-based NGO registered under the Society Registration Act, dedicated to empowering underprivileged communities through education, health awareness, cultural enrichment, and environmental stewardship.

Founded by Mrs. Rajni Joshi in 2017, the foundation was born out of a simple belief: that every child — regardless of their economic background — deserves access to quality education, joyful celebrations, and a dignified life.

What began as free basic education classes in Dwarka's Sector-6 has blossomed into a vibrant community initiative touching hundreds of lives every year through events, health camps, cultural programmes, and environmental drives.

Our Purpose

Mission, Vision & Values

Our Mission

To provide free education, healthcare awareness, and cultural enrichment to underprivileged children and communities in Delhi — empowering them to actualize their true potential.

Our Vision

A society where every child has equal access to education, every family is healthy, and every community is united by compassion, dignity, and shared purpose.

Our Values

Compassion · Integrity · Community · Inclusivity · Service. We believe in serving all humanity without discrimination, driven purely by love and the desire to give back.

Our Journey

9 Years of Impact

2017

Foundation Established

Mrs. Rajni Joshi foundedEKUM in Sector-6, Dwarka. The first free education classes for underprivileged children were launched.

2018–2019

Community Outreach Expands

Plantation drives, voting awareness campaigns, and Independence Day celebrations added to the programme. First partnerships with local councillors established.

2020–2021

Health Initiatives Launched

Yoga sessions, mental wellness programmes, and cancer awareness drives began in partnership with hospitals. The foundation continued serving through the pandemic.

2022–2023

Recognition & Growth

Received multiple awards for outstanding contributions to education and social welfare. Expanded initiatives to include cultural festivals and community celebrations.

2024–2025

500+ Children Impacted

Milestone: 500+ children supported. Monthly birthday celebrations, Holi, Cancer Awareness at Manipal Hospital, and Hanuman Jayanti events organized.

2026

New Horizons

Summer Sports Festival, continued education classes, community health drives, and this new digital presence — reaching more hearts than ever before.

What We Do

Our Core Initiatives

Free Education Classes

Basic education for underprivileged children — conducted by volunteers and foundation members in Sector-6, Dwarka.

Monthly Birthday Celebrations

Every child deserves to feel special. We celebrate monthly birthdays with snacks, gifts, and love for all children born that month.

Yoga & Health Drives

Community yoga sessions and health awareness campaigns including cancer awareness at Manipal Hospital, Dwarka.

Plantation Drives

Tree plantation, Say No to Plastic campaigns, and Earth Day awareness to protect the environment for future generations.

National Day Celebrations

Republic Day, Independence Day, and national festivals celebrated with patriotic fervour, flag hoisting, and cultural events.

Community Outreach

Engaging with local councillors, MPs, and community leaders to amplify our impact and bring resources to those in need.
